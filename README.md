### 👩‍💻 About Me

I am a **PhD-trained AI Research Scientist / Engineer** with postdoctoral experience in developing **trustworthy, explainable, and resource-efficient machine learning solutions** for safety-critical and real-world environments.

## 🔭 What I work on

- 🧠 **Trustworthy & explainable AI (XAI)** for safety-critical environments
- 🎯 **Uncertainty estimation & model calibration**
- 🔄 **Incremental learning** under concept drift in non-stationary settings
- 🔍 **Anomaly detection** on real-world sensor, IoT and video data
- 🪶 **Frugal AI**: model compression and deployment on resource-constrained devices
- 🎥 **Multimodal ML** (video, audio, text) and **human-AI teaming**
- 🤖 **Generative AI & LLM engineering** (RAG, AI agents)
